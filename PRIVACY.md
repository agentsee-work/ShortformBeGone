# Privacy policy

*Last updated 15 November 2026.*

ShortformBeGone collects no data.

- **What the filter sees.** To decide whether to block something, the content filter is shown the web address (in Safari and in-app browsers) or the hostname (for other apps' connections) of each new connection on your device. It compares that with the rule list on your device and answers "allow" or "block".
- **The Safari blocker.** The app gives Safari a list of rules. Safari applies them itself; the app and its extension are never shown the pages or addresses you visit.
- **The Safari guard.** A small script runs inside pages on youtube.com, instagram.com, facebook.com and snapchat.com only. It reads the address of the page, and if that address is a short-form section it takes you to the site's home page. Before leaving it pauses any video on that page and hides the page. It reads no page content, stores nothing and sends nothing.
- **What it keeps.** Nothing. It keeps no history and writes no record of what you visit. If you switch on `LogMatches` in your own profile, the name of the rule that matched (for example `youtube.com/shorts`) is written to your device's system log; the address you visited is not.
- **What it sends.** Nothing. The part of the filter that is shown addresses runs in a sandbox with no network access, and no part of it makes network connections. The app makes no network requests, has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.
- **What the app stores.** Which setup steps you have ticked, on your device only.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or delete.
